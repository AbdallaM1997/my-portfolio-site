SkillBanks VR

VRUnitySoft-skills training

A workplace conversation you have to handle yourself. You are briefed into a role, operations manager under pressure, then sat at a table with colleagues who push back while you answer. Every reply is a choice between four positions, and trust and collaboration meters along the top move with the one you take. It closes on a trade-off you have to commit to, weighting speed against quality against cost before you can finish.

  • Unity
  • VR headset
  • Character animation
  • Branching dialogue
  • Real-time 3D

Oncology VR for clinicians

VRUnityPharmaceutical clients

Treatment decisions rehearsed in the room where they get made. A case is set up around you, the population it applies to and the regimen on the table, then the patient stops tolerating the therapy and you have to choose what happens next, with the trial data standing beside you as survival curves rather than a slide. A second module is a reference wall you walk along: lung, oesophageal, colorectal and ovarian disease, each with its own panel of definitions and agents.

  • Unity
  • VR headset
  • Real-time 3D
  • Data visualisation
  • Character animation

Patient-education VR

VRUnityPharmaceutical clients

Branching scenarios that put a patient inside their own treatment journey: a dialysis centre, the daily burden of living with the condition, the fatigue that follows a session. Every branch is authored in the Unity Editor rather than hard-coded, so the client's medical team can change the script without a developer. A five-star rating at the end posts straight to an admin panel, so the people funding the project can see what audiences actually thought.

  • Unity
  • C#
  • 360° video
  • Meta Quest
  • ScriptableObjects
  • REST API

PPE and harness inspection
