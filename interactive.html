3D interactives for the browser

Web 3DUnityEducation

Self-contained 3D lessons a student drives themselves. A physics lab where a collision plays out and the data table fills in as it happens; a workshop where you choose the right tool and strip a brake assembly step by step, answering questions as you go; power stations and a wind turbine you orbit and tap to open up. Games for event floors and booths

Event activationUnity

Conference stands are a hostile place for software. Nobody reads instructions, nobody asks for help, and the thing has to survive eight hours of strangers hammering it. These are timed quizzes, scored challenges, lead-capture forms and photo activations built for that reality: readable across a room, self-resetting between players, and stable all day. Several run as two-player head-to-head to pull a crowd.

Welcome wall

Event installationUnityPharmaceutical clients

A guest writes a note by hand on a tablet at the entrance and it flies up onto an LED wall as a sticky card, joining everyone else's. Fire and rescue training simulation

Real-time 3DUnityIndustrial safety

A plant catches fire and you have to work the incident. One module puts an extinguisher in your hands: kill the power, keep your distance, pick the right agent for burning machinery. The other is casualty care: compressions, splinting a broken leg, getting someone onto a stretcher. A safety instructor briefs each task, a clock runs the whole time, and the scene is a working factory floor rather than a slide.

Vocational training games

2D gamesUnityTechnical education

Short activities that drill trade knowledge instead of testing it on paper. One runs a warehouse round where you match lamp types to the job they suit; the other is a spin-wheel on pipe materials, each answer opening a card on what the material is and where it belongs. Both keep score and lives, so a class can run them as a round rather than a quiz.

Conversational AI avatar

Real-time 3DConversational AI

A 3D presenter you talk to rather than watch. Speech goes in through the microphone, the reply comes back spoken and lip-synced on a character standing in front of you, and a transcript runs along the bottom so nothing is lost when the audio is unclear. It runs as a live loop rather than a rendered video, so the session goes wherever the person asking takes it.
