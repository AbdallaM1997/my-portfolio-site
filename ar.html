AR that runs in the browser

Web ARPharmaceutical clients

A doctor scans a QR code and 3D content appears on the table in front of them. No app store, no install, no IT approval, which is the whole point when your audience is a consultant with four minutes between patients. Built for product launches, step-by-step preparation guides, and walking through trial data as objects you move around rather than slides you sit through.

  • Web AR
  • 3D optimisation
  • Mobile browsers
  • QR entry

AR science textbook

Mobile ARUnity2020

A printed science textbook that runs its own experiments. Point a phone at a page and the apparatus builds itself on the desk: solutions to mix, heat to apply, a pendulum to time, a thermometer to read, a building to load floor by floor, a neuron and the nervous system to pull apart. Each one is a working simulation with its own controls and readouts, tracked off the page with Vuforia so the book stays the interface.

  • Unity
  • C#
  • Vuforia
  • Marker tracking
  • Android

AR story dioramas

Mobile ARUnity

Illustrated scenes that unfold off a flat page like a pop-up book: a reception desk, a workshop, a bank counter, each with characters acting out a short scene the reader walks around. Built so a printed page and a phone together do something neither can do alone.

  • Unity
  • C#
  • Marker tracking
  • Character animation

AR animal book

Mobile ARUnity2020

Point a phone at a printed page and the illustration stands up off the paper. Animals are instantiated at runtime with their own audio, and children can orbit the model, drop it onto any nearby surface, and photograph it. Built to bridge a physical textbook with 3D content rather than replace it.

  • Unity
  • C#
  • Vuforia
  • AssetBundles
  • Android

AR safety briefings

Mobile ARIndustrial training

Wind-turbine safety brought onto a desk. The turbine stands up at scale in front of you and each hazard opens as a panel against the part it belongs to: fire, working at height, moving machinery, lifting gear. Built so a crew can walk through a briefing together around one phone rather than a slide deck.

  • Unity
  • Surface tracking
  • Real-time 3D
  • Hotspots
